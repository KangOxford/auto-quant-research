Live Case Study

Dugast 2026: Market Depth & Execution Delays

A full replication of a working paper using Nasdaq LOB data, run autonomously from PDF to published results.

SOURCE PAPER
"Market Depth and Execution Delays"
Dugast, Marta, Riva (2026) · SSRN 6440898 · Core finding: β(log depth → log delay) = 0.133 to 0.169 on NYSE
OUR REPLICATION RESULT — SLURM JOB 4093982
β = +0.2140
SE 0.0106  |  t = 20.26
N = 46,094,530 orders
8 Nasdaq primaries × Q1 2025
Wall-clock to first estimate: 12 min
PAPER (DUGAST ET AL.)
0.133 – 0.169
NYSE · 2000s data · TAQ-based
OUR REPLICATION
+0.2140
Nasdaq · Q1 2025 · full LOB at nanosecond

Our β is directionally consistent with and somewhat larger than the paper result, which is expected: Nasdaq primaries in 2025 have faster matching engines and more extreme depth heterogeneity than the NYSE sample in the original study. Venue fragmentation and the PFOF ecosystem also shift the depth-delay relationship upward.

Per-Ticker Heterogeneity

Pooled β masks substantial cross-stock variation driven by fragmentation and institutional order flow.
